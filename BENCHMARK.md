# Benchmark and Rule-Derived Labels

The reported benchmark is generated by `app.agentic_system.build_evaluation_scenarios(count=180, seed=2026)`. It contains six hand-written core scenarios and 174 seeded mixed scenarios covering patient risk, flow pressure, staffing pressure, and bed capacity.

The generator uses explicit templates and hand-coded ranges. It does not load NHAMCS records, estimate distributions from NHAMCS, or sample patient values from NHAMCS. All included scenarios are synthetic.

Expected actions are software test oracles rather than independent clinical ground truth. No independent expert adjudication or inter-rater agreement study was performed.

## Action-label rules

The rules are implemented in `app.agentic_system._expected_actions_for_payload()`.

- `escalate_patient`: at least one patient has a composite oracle risk score of 0.58 or higher. ESI 1 or 2 alone is not sufficient.
- `reprioritize_queue`: queue length is at least 18, arrivals per hour are at least 20, average wait is at least 90 minutes, boarding count is at least 5, or staffing pressure is at least 0.95 while queue length is at least 12.
- `staffing_alert`: staffing pressure is at least 0.85.
- `reassign_bed`: occupancy is at least 0.95 with effective bed capacity at most 2, or free beds are at most 1 with a positive high-acuity bed gap.
- `admit_support`: at least one oracle escalation target exists and effective bed capacity is positive.
- `discharge_support`: occupancy is at least 0.85 and at least one discharge-ready bed exists.
- `monitor`: none of the other action conditions is met.

Effective bed capacity equals total beds minus occupied beds plus discharge-ready beds. Free beds are bounded below at zero. The high-acuity bed gap equals the number of ESI 1-2 patients minus available high-acuity beds, bounded below at zero.

## Composite escalation-target oracle

The benchmark oracle assigns ESI weights of 0.45, 0.30, 0.12, 0.03, and 0.01 for ESI levels 1-5. It adds 0.20 for systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg, 0.18 for oxygen saturation below 92%, 0.10 for respiratory rate above 24 or below 10 breaths/min, 0.08 for heart rate above 120 or below 45 beats/min, 0.16 for suspected sepsis, 0.06 for abnormal laboratory results, and 0.05 for age 75 years or older. The score is capped at 1.0, and every patient with a score of at least 0.58 is an escalation target.

## Oracle and validator distinction

The benchmark oracle and safety validator use separate patient-risk calculations. The oracle defines required escalation targets using the composite score above. The safety validator checks whether recommendations include every patient classified as high or critical by the patient-risk adapter. The two procedures identify the same target sets for all 180 reported scenarios, but their formulas are not identical and equivalence is not guaranteed beyond this benchmark.
